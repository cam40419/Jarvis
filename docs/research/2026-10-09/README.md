# Simon research package

Completed desk-research pass, **October 9, 2026**, with an **October 10, 2026 owner decision**
on baseline platform quality. Historical sources, prices and findings retain their original
date. This is a reviewable research package, not a competitive performance result or
approved product pivot. The [delivery roadmap](../../next-phases.md) and
[platform baseline quality plan](../../architecture/platform-baseline-quality-plan.md)
govern implementation.

Start with the [strategy and next sequence](strategy.md). The owner promoted five former
opportunity bundles into **required base platform quality**: change impact and bounded
repair; native editable delivery packets; effective review; reliable completion at honest
total cost; and authorized continuity. Their implementation must meet prespecified parity
for supported workflows against strong configured same-goal platforms and a competent
existing stack, under fair cost/privacy conditions. Narrow jobs are measurement fixtures
for the general platform. A failed implementation is redesigned; the requirement remains.

The October 9 research did not discover a proven empty market. Future branches, simulation,
generalized learning and other innovation beyond the five remain separate hypotheses.
The proposed 30% review-effort improvement is a research stretch target, not a baseline
prerequisite or a measured universal advantage. Complete the core gate before accepting
business tools one at a time.

## Evidence conventions and limitations

**Documented** means an inspected primary source describes behavior, not that its quality
was tested. **Announced** means preview/rollout or announcement-level evidence.
**Not established** means inspected sources do not establish a capability; it does not
mean the product lacks it. **Not applicable** identifies a different product role.
Each capability note is essential: content/file review can differ from exact-revision
independent quality acceptance or external-action permission.

Pricing preserves currencies, units, billing periods, credits and exceptions. Dynamic
render conflicts, inaccessible cards, beta features, sunset dates and license constraints
are explicit. Unlike credits were not normalized into task dollars; no numeric capability
rankings were invented. Community anecdotes inform interview hypotheses, not failure rates.

No paid competitor workflows, comparative trials, customer interviews or vendor contacts
were performed. A finite dated pass cannot exhaust every conceivable avenue or future
release; this covers material families and twenty themes, strong counterexamples and
three independent challenge perspectives. Next evidence requires real workflow/buyer tests.
