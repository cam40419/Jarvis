# Evaluation plan for accepted business work

Proposed on **October 9, 2026**. This plan operationalizes the [workflow/quality challenge](challenge-workflow-and-quality.md) and [systems/economics challenge](challenge-systems-and-economics.md). **No evaluation trials, paid calls, vendor account actions or user contacts were performed for this document.** Public product documentation is research evidence; it is not a comparative quality result.

**October 10, 2026 owner decision:** accurate change impact and bounded repair, native editable delivery packets, effective human review, reliable completion at honest total cost and authorized continuity are required base platform quality. Their acceptance and the comparative gate are defined in the [platform baseline quality plan](../../architecture/platform-baseline-quality-plan.md). This document is the comparison protocol, not evidence that the requirements have passed.

The required question is whether each declared supported workflow reaches a usable, current, editable and authorized result at least as well as the strongest eligible configured same-goal comparators and a competent existing stack, under prespecified quality, review-effort, recovery, cost and privacy conditions. Narrow workflows are demanding measurement fixtures for Simon's general platform. The proposed **30% reduction in active human effort with no quality loss** remains an optional analyst-selected research stretch target; it is neither a baseline prerequisite nor a validated universal advantage.

## Sequence and present boundaries

**Stage 0: current bounded text screening, then the complete baseline core gate.** Use small synthetic fixtures to evaluate the existing text maker/reviewer/owner loop, exact revision acceptance, linked repair, model reservations and uncertainty handling. Keep automatic review within its UTF-8/evidence bounds; respect current artifact/file limits. Derive initial text-only brief/specification/report packets from the six jobs below. This screening does not complete native creative production, a live supplier workflow or the expanded core gate.

Core acceptance is the authoritative prerequisite: resource/entitlement enforcement, administration, security/concurrency/recovery/restore, PostgreSQL acceptance, usability, appropriately authorized bounded model-quality evaluation and all five baseline outcomes plus their comparative gate. Generic/reference operations, declared native imports, delivery manifests and validators must establish the core contracts before production business adapters follow. Research, software and creative fixtures cover the declared supported profiles; every material evidence gap remains open. See the [canonical quality plan](../../architecture/platform-baseline-quality-plan.md), [roadmap](../../next-phases.md), [artifacts](../../runbooks/native-artifacts.md), [execution](../../runbooks/native-execution.md) and [models](../../runbooks/project-models.md).

**Stage 1: one accepted business tool after the core gate.** Select one recurring fixture and one narrow operation that materially helps it. The adapter inherits the five baseline contracts; accept its object scopes, editable format, renderer/checks, monetary accounting and interrupted-effect recovery. Screen its five variants before adding another tool. A file/generator or Git operation is a candidate; this is not permission for arbitrary local shell, desktop automation or real publication.

**Stage 2: broader workflow confirmation.** Only supported, accepted operations enter cross-application comparisons. Full six-job confirmation requires corresponding capabilities; unsupported creative work remains a proposed future test. Do not silently substitute a polished screenshot for the promised native output.

Start cheaply: a fixture/rubric rehearsal without model calls; ten synthetic Stage-0 screening cases covering two text jobs and all five variants; extend to declared native, software/research/creative and continuity profiles for the baseline core gate; then five Stage-1 cases for the selected accepted adapter. Repeat failed or ambiguous cases after a documented fix. A separately scoped, capped paid screen can follow once the local protocol works. Freeze screening findings and use held-out fixtures for confirmation; a screen is not marketing evidence or the complete baseline acceptance matrix.

## Six jobs and their ground truth

| Job                       | Bounded accepted result                                                                                    | Ground truth and indispensable check                                                                                    |
| ------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Product launch            | Approved product record → editable campaign master, SKU sheet, product-page draft and publication proposal | Exact variant, material, dimensions and price; visual/product identity agrees with text                                 |
| Supplier handoff          | Existing expert-approved specification → structured summary, cost sheet and staged supplier package        | Units, specification revision, calculation and recipient; technical expert remains responsible for physical feasibility |
| Agency rebrand            | Client guidelines/assets → native design, deck and website change package                                  | Approved wording/assets, client separation, permitted audience and intended native edit                                 |
| Evidence-to-pitch         | Approved commercial evidence → calculation workbook, cited deck and proposal                               | Traceable figures, correct formulas/units, rendered figures match native calculations                                   |
| Digital product launch    | Brief → working app, editable visuals, help text and release packet                                        | Actual required behavior, mobile rendering, visual consistency and exact release revision                               |
| Recurring campaign report | Metric definitions/data → calculations, deck and proposed next campaign                                    | Date windows, denominators, reproducible computations and supported conclusions                                         |

A domain expert writes the expected material facts and failure severity before execution. Creative preference has a separate rubric for the declared audience/brand; it cannot offset a wrong SKU or unsupported claim. Native format, dependencies and a designated edit are specified in each fixture. Manufacturing readiness and rights clearance are not inferred from generated imagery.

## Five variants and repeat design

Each job has five independently reset variants, making **6 × 5 = 30 cases**. Confirmation proposes **three stochastic repeats of each case: 90 trials per platform/configuration**. Three arms would therefore require 270 trials, before setup or reruns. This is a future workload and budget proposal, not an instruction to spend now.

| Variant          | Fixture intervention                                                                  | What must be observed                                                                          |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Normal           | Clear objective, consistent sources and valid files                                   | Usable output with appropriate questions and no unnecessary rework                             |
| Seeded defect    | Held-out wrong variant, unit, claim, formula or layout                                | Material defect detected before acceptance; correct explanation without invented findings      |
| Source change    | Accepted output followed by one authoritative material change and one irrelevant edit | Correct affected work reopened/repaired; unrelated work preserved                              |
| Owner correction | One authorized local correction after candidate creation                              | Intended correction applied to exact targets without collateral regressions                    |
| Interruption     | Controlled stop/lost response around a bounded operation                              | Authority and unknown liabilities preserved; externally observed state reconciled before retry |

Use the same fixture per repeat with fresh sessions and recorded stochastic settings; this separates run variation from fixture variation. Use additional held-out briefs/seeds later to assess generalization. Independent variants do not inherit earlier answers or corrections. Inject faults through a harness/local mock where possible; real-system fault injection needs its own accepted scope.

Choose one interruption type for each job's fixed fixture and keep it identical across its three stochastic repeats. Cover pre-dispatch termination, lost reply after a possible effect, worker/lease loss, revoked grants/readers, changed destinations, simultaneous reservations and restoration through separately counted core micro-probes. This preserves the meaning of repeat consistency in the 90-trial quality matrix.

## Fair baselines and candidate selection

Compare Simon with **a competent practitioner using an appropriate normal stack**: existing PM/file history plus the best relevant creative suite/proofing tool and a strong assistant. Also compare the strongest eligible configured platforms pursuing the same job, selected from documented fit and a setup/screening pass rather than convenience. The existing stack can be stronger than any isolated product. A narrow fixture determines measurement, not the platform's core product scope.

Candidate sets, subject to verified account availability and actual job fit:

- Product/rebrand work: Canva, Figma or Adobe plus Filestage/Ziflow/Frame.io and a capable work agent.
- Digital product launch: Cursor, Devin, Codex or Claude Code with native review/tests; Replit/Lovable/Bolt/Base44 where the deployment job fits.
- Evidence/report work: ChatGPT Work, Claude apps/Cowork, Manus/Genspark or the buyer's current Office/data stack; specialist evidence tools where eligible.
- Supplier handoff: an experienced practitioner and their existing approved specification/CAD/PLM tools; no general agent is presumed to replace the technical expert.

The [market notes](execution-and-creative-platforms.md) establish candidate capabilities, not winners. Choose/freeze configurations before held-out tests; allow experienced operators equal setup time. Enable documented review, memory, templates and automation that buyers would reasonably use. Record edition, feature flags, tool versions and date. Unavailable features and APIs are evidence gaps or applicability limits, not universal product failures.

Give all arms equivalent sources, business goals, budgets and allowed effects, while allowing native strengths. Freeze wall-time, paid-attempt and repair ceilings in each fixture; record every attempt within a trial instead of selecting a successful rerun. Do not demand identical internal architecture or models. Record manual actions and custom scripts. Separate one-time configuration from recurring operation, and randomize platform/operator order to reduce learning effects. Treat materially changed configurations as new arms.

**Prespecify the parity floor per workflow before confirmation.** Record indispensable correctness and native-usability criteria, material-defect/change-coverage margins, review-effort limits, completion/recovery expectations, authorized-continuity invariants and the acceptable cost envelope. Select the strongest relevant eligible comparator on each material measure; do not choose one weak all-purpose rival or average away a baseline deficit. Owner-approved margins must be explicit and justified before results are known; superiority is a separate claim.

Compare matched capability/privacy profiles. A local-only requirement is tested against eligible local/private alternatives, with hardware, setup and support included; hosted capabilities can be tested under a separately declared permitted-data profile. Report ineligible or unavailable arms and unresolved costs honestly. A privacy restriction, different model or hidden third-party bill cannot silently favor either Simon or a rival. Approval/authority and material-quality failures do not become acceptable because an arm is cheaper or faster.

## Rubric, blind review and hard gates

Record first-candidate and final-after-repair results separately. Two reviewers, blinded to platform identity where feasible, inspect actual delivered files using a fixed rubric. A third adjudicates material disagreement. Cosmetic clues may reveal a tool; record broken blinding rather than claiming perfection. Makers and operator scripts do not receive hidden defect answers.

Each case gets discrete results for required facts, calculations, source support/freshness, native usability, visual/render correctness, change coverage, repair locality and delivery identity. Record false acceptance, correctly found defects, false rejections, missed impacts and unnecessary invalidations. Explain evidence, severity and unresolved checks instead of compressing everything into one favorable score.

Native checks include opening in the intended application, modifying the designated field/object, saving, reopening and rendering. Inspect formulas, layers/objects, fonts, links, hidden content, pagination/crops and destination export where relevant. Downloading PPTX establishes a format option, not editability; Gemini Notebook's current help supports PPTX download, while actual structure/fidelity remains to be tested. [Current help](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16757456).

**Hard failures gate expansion regardless of averages:** reviewed bytes differ from accepted/released bytes; revoked authority is used; private/revoked evidence is disclosed through a derived artifact; unsupported writes occur; duplicate effects are retried without reconciliation; concurrent admission violates a promised ceiling; restore loses essential state; or a bounded interruption leaves the workspace indefinitely starved. Stop the affected operation, preserve its record and diagnose it. Zero observed failures is not proof these failures are impossible.

A failing baseline implementation or missing evidence keeps core acceptance open. Repair or redesign the implementation while retaining all five general platform requirements. Preserve failed cases and the registered scope; removing a failed fixture or post hoc narrowing does not establish parity. A fixture failure is not a reason to discard change handling, editable delivery, effective review, reliable completion or authorized continuity.

An unknown external outcome is not automatically a failure: the rubric expects honest uncertainty and safe reconciliation. It becomes a failure when the system falsely declares success, clears liability without evidence, or blindly repeats a potentially completed effect.

## Human effort and complete economics

Time active briefing/setup, source checks, decision review, correction, recovery, handoff and expert cleanup. Include concierge/developer intervention. Record interruption count, question usefulness, repair cycles, elapsed time and queue waiting separately; a fast worker can still create an expensive owner queue.

Bill every incurred attempt: successful, rejected, abandoned, canceled after dispatch, review, retries and discarded creative variants. Capture provider/model/tool charges, GPU/CPU/runtime, storage/renders/backups, connector/export fees and taxes where material. Report customer-BYOK charges and local compute instead of declaring them free.

Show three views: marginal incurred execution charges; allocated subscriptions/setup/support; and total cost including human time using an explicit rate range. Allocation assumptions and hardware depreciation/electricity estimates stay visible. Unsettled liabilities are separate amounts/ranges with their evidence and reconciliation status, never silently zero. If a vendor hides task costs, report missing attribution plus observed account balance/invoices; do not invent conversions between credits.

## Fixture and harness record schema

Use a documented schema before running trials. References to secrets are identifiers only; raw credentials never enter exports.

| Record        | Required fields                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fixture       | schema/version, case/job/variant IDs, brief, objective, source snapshots/hashes, authority/policy revisions, format/render profile, intended edit, dependencies, budget/allowed effects, wall-time/attempt/repair ceilings, hidden expected facts/defects, severity and rubric version |
| Configuration | platform/edition/version/date, agent/model routes, stochastic settings, tool/account scopes, renderer/validator versions, memory reset, operator, setup minutes and configuration digest                                                                                               |
| Trial         | case/config/repeat/seed IDs, timestamps, attempts/events, steering/questions, source reads/omissions, candidate revisions/hashes, checks/findings, owner decisions and applicable authority                                                                                            |
| Effect/cost   | request/idempotency identity, destination/account/object, accepted payload hash, observed receipt/state, retry/compensation, reservations, settled charges, unknown liabilities and supporting evidence                                                                                |
| Review        | blinded artifact ID, reviewer, per-criterion finding/severity/evidence, native-edit/render results, acceptance/repair history, active minutes, disagreement/adjudication                                                                                                               |
| Summary       | applicability, first/final success, hard-gate outcome, incurred/allocated cost, missing data, human effort, unresolved state, protocol deviation and exclusion reason                                                                                                                  |

Pin fixtures and harness versions. Keep answer keys access-separated, timestamp fault injection, and retain all raw trials. A material fixture correction requires regrading every affected arm under the same version, with old results retained.

## Analysis and decision targets

Report per-job and per-variant counts, first-pass/final success, median/tail human minutes, cost and uncertainty. Primary effort outcome is paired recurring active minutes against the strong existing stack; keep failures and abandoned trials in the denominator.

Use paired confidence intervals with the fixture as the comparison unit, and account for repeated trials/nested jobs. Do not treat all 90 observations as independent customers. With only six job families, report sensitivity to clustering and avoid precise general market claims. Pre-register quality margins and sample-size reasoning after screening variance is known. “No significant difference” does not establish no quality loss.

Report **pass^1** and consistent success across all three repeats (**pass^3**); this is different from best-of-three success. Generalize to pass^k only with enough repeats and an explicit estimator. A lucky accepted output or selected rerun is not reliability.

**Required progression:** meet the prespecified per-workflow comparative parity floors on material quality, native usability, source/change correctness, human review burden, reliable completion and authorized continuity; pass the mandatory hard gates; satisfy the declared all-in cost/privacy conditions; and preserve unresolved evidence as open work. Confirmation must disclose confidence limits. If they do not establish the declared floor, collect evidence or redesign the implementation; no aggregate score substitutes for acceptance.

**Separate research stretch:** a 30% recurring active-effort reduction with no quality loss may be tested after or alongside the parity assessment. Failure to achieve that stretch does not itself fail the required baseline or justify removing a capability. A passing fixture does not establish universal superiority. Repeated willingness to use/pay is a later commercial study, not a condition for implementing the owner's baseline requirements or something implied by technical passing.

## Primary evaluation precedents

WorkArena offers browser task validation, while tau-bench supplies policy/tool/user simulation and explicit task evaluation; its current repository has evolved beyond tau2, so pin versions. StagedWorkspace motivates native/parsed/diff identity checks. Trace2Flow motivates testing understandable post-task evidence, while its study is not evidence for Simon. Reuse methods and applicable fixtures rather than borrowing their scores. [WorkArena](https://github.com/ServiceNow/WorkArena), [tau-bench](https://github.com/sierra-research/tau2-bench), [StagedWorkspace](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18050), [Trace2Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13136).
