# Execution agents and creative production platforms

Observed **October 9, 2026**. The accompanying [structured map](execution-and-creative-platforms.json) contains **30 profiles and 91 distinct official source URLs**. Profiles distinguish documented behavior, announcements/limited rollout and unanswered questions. Documentation establishes what a vendor describes, not independent reliability or comparative output quality. No accounts, paid tools, installations or production workloads were exercised.

## Finding

Simon faces competition from complete work products, local agent systems, professional creative tools, and combinations of those products. Boards, multiple agents, model choice, schedules, memory, screenshots, checkpoints, spend controls and approval are established capabilities. Revision-linked creative review is also established. Simon's plausible opportunity is **less coordination and correction across a complete, changing business outcome**, with usable native files, explicit authority, observed delivery and understandable total cost. This is a hypothesis, not a proven moat.

Current Simon has bounded text artifact review, immutable versions and exact-version owner acceptance. It does not yet have visual/physical-product validation, accepted production creative connectors, arbitrary local tool execution, full compute/storage charging or a production hosted service. The repository's [README](../../../README.md), [artifact runbook](../../runbooks/native-artifacts.md) and [roadmap](../../next-phases.md) describe these boundaries. A stronger internal invariant does not establish a better customer outcome.

## How this pass was iterated

1. **Breadth:** surveyed local agents, coding agents, shared workspaces, application builders and creative production.
2. **Challenge:** searched for capabilities that would disprove easy differentiation claims. OpenClaw already has Workboard/Lobster/recovery; Cursor produces review evidence; OpenHands offers an enterprise control plane.
3. **Freshness:** reopened current docs/pricing where older articles conflicted. Devin's current tiers differ from older ACU-era pages; Cowork's newer cloud schedules supersede desktop-awake guidance; Figma Design agent/Weave tools became generally available October 6.
4. **Missing-family audit:** added creative proofing/DAM. Frame.io/Workfront, Filestage, Ziflow and Frontify make an independent review workspace and brand memory much more crowded.

This is a bounded capability map, not a claim to have enumerated every possible product or idea. Remaining unknowns are listed below.

## Local ownership and personal-agent ecosystems

**OpenClaw — direct competitor for technical users.** Its optional Workboard tracks tasks and runs; Lobster provides resumable, explicitly approved pipelines. Restart recovery preserves requester authority and treats interrupted uncertain actions cautiously. Its security boundary is a trusted operator/team, rather than hostile tenants. These are substantial documented systems, not proof every installation enables them correctly. Lobster is disabled in sandbox contexts and possession of its resume token matters; evaluate the exact configured execution path. [Workboard](https://docs.openclaw.ai/plugins/workboard), [Lobster](https://docs.openclaw.ai/tools/lobster), [recovery](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/restart-recovery), [security](https://docs.openclaw.ai/gateway/security).

OpenClaw's provenance documentation describes bounded receipts and forgetting markers, while warning that its audit storage is not a compliance archive. Its locally separated agents/workspaces already weaken provider-neutral ownership positioning. A Simon opportunity might be easier mixed-trust collaboration and cross-deliverable change control; whether OpenClaw extensions already supply a sufficient equivalent remains untested. Software is MIT/open source; model, hosting and maintenance costs still exist. [Overview](https://docs.openclaw.ai/), [agent separation](https://docs.openclaw.ai/concepts/multi-agent), [provenance](https://docs.openclaw.ai/start/why-openclaw/provenance).

**Hermes Agent — direct personal-agent competitor.** Persistent memory, skills, routines and specialist bot profiles address a long-lived assistant rather than disposable chats. Nous Portal bundles model/tool access, reducing setup friction. Multi-tenant artifact acceptance and total economic controls were not established in reviewed sources. Open-source ownership does not imply all connected services stay local. Portal dollar tiers were not verified. [Docs](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/), [Bot Mode](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/bot-mode), [Portal](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/integrations/nous-portal).

**OpenCode — adjacent coding tool and possible engine.** A local, provider-configurable coding experience with granular tool permissions is already available. It is more relevant as a reusable execution specialist than as a complete business-project comparator. Permission approval is not a verdict on deliverable correctness. [Overview](https://opencode.ai/docs/), [permissions](https://opencode.ai/docs/permissions/).

## Coding agents and general agent work

**OpenHands — strong control-plane threat and potential component.** OSS supports local single-user workflows; managed/enterprise offerings add isolated execution, model routing, budgets, context, observability and BYO agents. Enterprise deployments include private/VPC configurations. Action-risk analyzers and confirmation policies are documented separately from output review. Simon should avoid competing on a generic model-neutral agent control plane without demonstrating a materially easier business outcome. Individual cloud platform access is free with limits and BYOK/at-cost provider usage; Enterprise is custom-priced. [Pricing](https://www.openhands.dev/pricing), [enterprise](https://www.openhands.dev/enterprise), [SDK](https://docs.openhands.dev/sdk), [security](https://docs.openhands.dev/sdk/guides/security).

**Devin — autonomous software delivery and visible evidence.** Current docs cover background issue work, cloud handoff, browser/terminal execution, review, tests and recordings. Current pricing is USD $20/month Pro, $200 Max, and Teams $80/month base plus $40 per full developer seat; daily/weekly quotas and extra API-priced usage apply. Do not reuse older Core/Team ACU sticker prices as current truth. Compare software tasks against Devin's configured review/testing workflow, not a bare chatbot. [Introduction](https://docs.devin.ai/get-started/devin-intro), [testing/recordings](https://docs.devin.ai/work-with-devin/testing-and-recordings), [review](https://docs.devin.ai/work-with-devin/devin-review), [pricing](https://devin.ai/pricing).

**Cursor — evidence-producing agents with repeatable automation.** Cloud agents work in parallel isolated environments, test applications, and return screenshots/videos/logs; automations respond to schedules and application events. Its enterprise self-hosted workers place tools on customer hardware, but orchestration/inference and some outputs still use Cursor cloud. This distinction matters when comparing local ownership. Pro is USD $20/month; Teams $40/user/month. Cloud model use is API-priced with spend-limit setup. [Cloud agents](https://cursor.com/docs/cloud-agent), [automations](https://cursor.com/docs/cloud-agent/automations), [self-hosted boundary](https://cursor.com/docs/cloud-agent/self-hosted), [pricing](https://cursor.com/pricing).

**Claude Code — capable specialist, with scoped experimental limitations.** Agent teams coordinate a shared task list and teammate messages. Current team documentation labels this experimental and lists resumption/state limitations. File checkpoints support rewind; they should not be presented as universal rollback of shell commands or external actions. These limitations apply to the documented team/checkpoint features, not to the entire Claude ecosystem. [Teams](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/agent-teams), [checkpointing](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/checkpointing).

**Claude Cowork — direct general-work competitor.** The newer July 7 cloud/web/mobile update documents work and schedules continuing while devices are off, with rollout restrictions. Current product pages include multi-step files/tools/browser work and parallel chunks. Older desktop-awake-only scheduling instructions should not be cited as its universal current limitation. Pro is USD $20/month or $200/year; Max 5x is $100/month and Max 20x $200/month, with shared usage. Exact revision acceptance semantics remain unestablished, not absent. [Cowork](https://claude.com/product/cowork), [July update](https://claude.com/resources/articles/cowork-web-mobile), [pricing](https://claude.com/pricing).

**OpenAI Codex — adjacent specialist execution with ongoing goals.** Current official docs describe long-running goals, steering, parallel work, local/cloud surfaces and approvals. Local OS sandboxing and cloud controls have distinct scopes. Simon gains little from rebuilding the coding loop; an accepted specialist adapter may be more useful. Work/Codex plan usage is shared; Plus is USD $20/month and current Pro tiers $100/$200/$500, with API and credits separate. [Long-running work](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/long-running-work), [approvals/security](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-approvals-security), [features](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/features), [pricing](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/pricing).

**ChatGPT Work, Projects and Space — direct workspace competition.** Shared project instructions/sources and collaborative pages connect research, documents, code and apps. Cloud Team Tasks differ from local scheduled tasks that need an available device. Access to a source does not mean a summary copied to a shared page preserves that source's audience. **ChatGPT Workspace Agents — direct shared enterprise workflow competition.** April's official cookbook describes research-preview agents combining shared skills, connections and schedules. Current general availability and incremental per-run prices were not settled. **ChatGPT dots — major new direct autonomy threat.** Official September 29 material and current docs describe gradually rolling-out cloud agents with continuing responsibilities, background workers, memory and their own computers. Action auto-review also differs from quality acceptance. Eligibility and exact incremental economics remain unknown here. ## Hypotheses worth testing, and what would falsify them

| Hypothesis                                     | Smallest convincing result                                                                                                | Falsifier                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Acceptance across a complete commercial packet | A changed product fact updates the editable design, SKU sheet, website and supplier handoff, preserving version decisions | Existing PM + proofing + strong agent is as easy, or false change alarms overwhelm users        |
| Cost per accepted deliverable                  | Show failed attempts, review, repair, tool/compute/storage and unresolved charges for the same result                     | Owners do not act on the information, or savings are outweighed by collection/integration costs |
| Business truth plus creative quality           | Catch inconsistent product imagery/specifications and unsupported price/copy claims, using expert-approved rules          | Only cosmetic issues are caught, or physical/technical errors remain hidden                     |
| Domain-specific change control                 | Reopen just the affected approvals when a source or native file changes                                                   | Everything gets re-reviewed, or important dependent outputs escape                              |
| Vendor-agent participation                     | Use strong coding/design agents while one owner-facing result remains portable and reviewable                             | APIs/rights/cost visibility make integration brittle or more work than direct vendor use        |
| Attention-aware review                         | Reduce active expert minutes without increasing accepted defects                                                          | Fewer prompts merely hide unresolved work or quality uncertainty                                |
| Portable accepted work                         | A second authorized person can resume from native sources, previews, evidence and outstanding obligations                 | The bundle is an unreadable archive or requires specialist reconstruction                       |

These are proposed experiments. No competitive quality scores or claims of superior reliability follow from feature presence.

## Uncertainties and exclusions

- **Not hands-on validated:** output quality, failure recovery, exact approval invalidation, account permission boundaries, supported native exports and marginal per-task economics.
- **Prices with weaker retrieval:** Replit/Lovable/Filestage current paid dollar cards were unavailable; Weave billing figures conflicted; Firefly localized. Frame.io's initial open failed, then a later direct reopen succeeded. Team totals require exact licenses, billing term and model/tool activity.
- **Preview/rollout:** dots eligibility, Workspace Agents GA/per-run pricing, Cowork cloud rollout and Frontify MCP/Specs status require account-specific confirmation. An announcement is not universal availability.
- **Interoperability:** a documented API/MCP does not grant permission to automate every subscription UI, export all native structure, inspect all usage, or embed/resell the vendor service. Each accepted operation needs its own contract.
- **Scope boundary:** deep research/report generation overlaps other team notes. Apparel CAD/PLM/tech-pack tools need a dedicated specialist pass before manufacturing claims. Concept images are not production proof.
- **Non-equivalence:** a workflow framework can implement an acceptance gate, while a finished proofing product already offers it. Simon must compare practical setup, review burden, correction and delivery, not theoretical absence.
