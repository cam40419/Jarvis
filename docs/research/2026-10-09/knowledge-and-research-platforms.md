# Knowledge and research platforms

Observed October 9, 2026. Five profiles supplement the [market strategy](strategy.md)
and [structured data](knowledge-and-research-platforms.json). Documentation establishes
advertised behavior, not comparative performance. Unknown means not established in the
inspected sources. No paid account or competitor production workflow was tested.

## Perplexity Computer and Projects

**Relationship: direct.** Current Projects replaces Spaces with shared ongoing work,
persistent files, connected project sources and accumulated Brain context. This is a
closer comparator than a search assistant. Exact output acceptance and source permission
rechecks still need a configured trial.
[Projects](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/products/projects).

Portable Computer weakens generic local-model positioning. Official documentation describes
a local harness, inference, sandbox and scheduled work on supported Windows/Linux devices,
with approval before cloud escalation. Current requirements include supported NVIDIA hardware
and at least 24GB GPU memory; supported AMD devices are listed in the Windows subsection.
Model support varies by device. Zero local token charges do not establish zero hardware,
support or total operating cost.
[Portable Computer](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/products/portable-computer).

Enterprise Academy documents seat allocations, organization pools, member/group limits,
refill ceilings and pause/resume when credits run out. These are substantial cost controls;
project dollar-liability accounting and uncertain-effect recovery remain separate questions.
[Credit management](https://www.perplexity.ai/en-GB/hub/academy/managing-computer-credits).

**Pricing caution.** The enterprise URL redirects to a render of individual prices:
$17/month Pro and $167/month Max billed annually, with 10,000 monthly Max credits and
separate bonuses. Enterprise seat dollars remain unresolved. The subscription guide
separately identifies Brain as research preview in relevant packaging; marketing alone
does not establish equal access on every plan.
[Pricing](https://www.perplexity.ai/enterprise/pricing),
[subscription guide](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/11187416-which-perplexity-subscription-plan-is-right-for-you).

**Discriminator.** Change a source, correct a deliverable and interrupt a continuing
project. Measure usable results and owner effort including setup. Persistent files,
research and local/cloud routing alone are poor headline differentiators.

## Dust

**Relationship: direct.** Pods put conversations, files and tasks into shared human/agent
workspaces. Both can create, assign, start and complete tasks, with linked conversations
for steering. External synchronization requires clear authority to avoid loops.
[Pods](https://docs.dust.tt/docs/user-documentation/pods/overview),
[tasks](https://docs.dust.tt/docs/user-documentation/pods/tasks).

Members can work on tasks and upload files; editors administer membership/settings. This
does not prove that derived memory retains every original source's disclosure boundary.
[Roles](https://docs.dust.tt/docs/user-documentation/pods/members-and-roles).

Frames support interactive file preview/export/share. They are snapshots; scheduled refresh
can overwrite the existing file. Immutable independent acceptance history is not established.
[Frames](https://docs.dust.tt/docs/user-documentation/pods/frames).

**Pricing.** Fetched locale uses euros: Free includes 500 lifetime credits; Pro is EUR30
monthly or EUR24/month annually, with 8,000 credits/seat/month; Max is EUR150 or EUR120 with
40,000 credits. Tax excluded. API billing separately uses $0.01/credit. Optional overages
can be capped; in-flight responses may finish beyond quota. US/EU residency and enterprise
single-tenant options are listed. Do not normalize mixed credits/currencies into task cost.
[Pricing](https://dust.tt/home/pricing).

**Discriminator.** Compare current brief/evidence handling and correction-to-acceptance
effort against a configured Pod. Shared agent/human board operations already match much
of Simon's premise. Crash recovery, unresolved charges and source revocation remain questions.

## Glean Agents

**Relationship: enterprise adjacent.** Auto agents select their path; Workflow agents use
configured steps/branches/tools. Delegation and selective working memory support research
across systems. These are workflow/context components, not proof of creative quality.
[How agents work](https://docs.glean.com/agents/how-agents-work).

Independent agents use attributed profiles and approved connected identities without an
active individual session. The current page explicitly labels them beta and deployment
dependent. It describes owners, administrator boundaries and human approval before learning
durable procedures. Do not label every independent capability generally available.
[Independent agents](https://docs.glean.com/agents/independent-agents).

Commercial dollars, project hard budgets, uncertain-tool recovery and output-file exact
acceptance were not established. An older action documentation path fetched an empty page;
its search snippet is not current capability proof.

**Discriminator.** Test revoked-source disclosure, recurring evidence-backed decisions and
second-person handoff. For enterprise buyers, existing retrieval, identity, procurement
and support relationships can outweigh individual feature differences.

## Hebbia

**Relationship: specialist adjacent.** The June disclosure documents project history,
API/MCP access, source references in generated PowerPoint and Tick-and-Tie checks matching
presentation metrics to reference data. Optional pre-run source review adds another control.
This is a direct counterexample to claimed novelty in cited documents or figure verification.
Commercial dollars, uncertain-effect recovery and version-bound human acceptance were not
established in this source.
[June disclosure](https://www.hebbia.com/blog/whats-new-june-disclosure-2026).

**Discriminator.** Compare a specialist's real figure-validation process. Simon needs a
measured benefit in a supported production workflow, changing requirements or handoff.
A broad research/slide contest can favor a specialist; that is useful evidence.

## Gemini Notebook formerly NotebookLM

**Relationship: source-synthesis substitute.** Current official help now uses Gemini Notebook
and describes source-grounded research and generated briefing/study formats.
[Overview](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16164461?hl=en).

Drive imports automatically synchronize. Losing Drive access makes the source inaccessible
and excluded from future use. This establishes meaningful freshness/permission handling;
reassessing previously generated derivatives remains a separate question.
[Sources](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16215270?hl=en).

Slide decks support presentation, PDF/PPTX download and revision instructions. Official help
currently says source documents are not considered during slide revisions. Exported native
object editability has not been tested. This is a precise limitation, rather than claiming
that slides cannot be edited or downloaded as PowerPoint.
[Slide decks](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16757456?hl=en).

Free/higher plan limits exist; commercial dollars were not established from help. Test a
source change followed by slide correction and inspect the actual exported file. A notebook
plus human review may already satisfy many buyers.
[Plans](https://support.google.com/gemininotebook/answer/16213268?hl=en).

## Research prior art and customer evidence

StagedWorkspace binds native files, parsed records and review views to versions/hashes;
its review-diff experiment supplies methodological precedent. Trace2Flow studies editable
graphs reconstructed after agent tasks, with improved understanding/error finding in a small
study. These are preprints, not evidence of market adoption or Simon's performance.
[StagedWorkspace](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.18050),
[Trace2Flow](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13136).

An inspected Manus thread includes credit complaints and a heavy user's favorable account.
It supports interviewing buyers about predictability, not estimating failure prevalence
or establishing product prices. No representative customer research occurred.
[Mixed user reports](https://www.reddit.com/r/ManusOfficial/comments/1wtj7we/credits_are_melting_away_very_fast/).

Source research, collaborative projects and local inference already have strong alternatives.
The opportunity remains a measured joined production workflow for a named buyer.
