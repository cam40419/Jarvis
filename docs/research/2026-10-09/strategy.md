# Simon competitive research and product strategy

Research date: **October 9, 2026**. Status: researched recommendations and testable
hypotheses, with the **October 10, 2026 owner decision** below. This is not a measured
product ranking. Historical vendor findings and prices retain their research date.

## Owner decision: five capabilities belong in the base platform

On **October 10, 2026**, the owner required accurate change impact and bounded repair,
native editable delivery packets, effective human review, reliable completion at honest
total cost, and authorized continuity across people, tools and providers as **base platform
quality**. They are required outcomes, rather than optional innovation bets or a sales pivot.
The [platform baseline quality plan](../../architecture/platform-baseline-quality-plan.md)
defines their implementation and acceptance alongside the [delivery roadmap](../../next-phases.md).
This decision promotes the requirements; it does not establish that they are implemented
or accepted today.

For each declared supported workflow, prespecify the comparison and parity floor against
strong configured platforms pursuing the same goal and a competent practitioner using an
appropriate existing stack. Quality, native usability, change correctness, review burden,
recovery and authorized continuity must meet that floor under fair cost and privacy
conditions. A failed design requires repair or redesign; the required capability remains.
Broader superiority claims need evidence for their stated scope.

## Recommendation

Keep Simon's general platform ambition and complete the five baseline capabilities as part
of the core acceptance gate. Use demanding recurring workflows to measure **keeping related
deliverables correct, editable and usable as requirements change, with effective owner
supervision**. A source-backed product/campaign packet from the stdout pilot is a useful
measurement fixture, not a reduction of the platform to a clothing-brand or agency product.
Supported capability profiles and product claims should name the outputs, correction
bounds, privacy conditions and handoff that have actually been accepted.

This pass does not establish an uncontested feature category. Shared agent teams, project
memory, workflow durability, budgets, generated files, quality checks and version-specific
approvals already have strong comparators. The baseline obligation is a joined workflow
with demonstrated quality and economics. A competitor can plausibly configure the same bundle, and a
competent human using existing applications is an equally important baseline.

The proposed experience is concrete: a price or material changes; Simon identifies affected
work, proposes bounded corrections, checks the actual deliverable, asks the owner only for
the decisions that matter and hands over the intended revision. Every part has limits:
semantic impact is not guaranteed by a hash, a second model can share an error, and an
accepted file is not permission to publish it. Simon should earn that promise through
the [evaluation plan](evaluation-plan.md).

## What Simon actually has

The repository already implements shared projects/boards and scoped agent identities,
bounded intake/staffing with a separate review call, model configuration/qualification,
encrypted project keys, inference reservations and unknown-charge accounting, PostgreSQL
execution checkpoints/waits and immutable artifacts with owner correction/acceptance.
The new artifact slice records exact revision/hash decisions and linked bounded repairs.
These are useful foundations, described in the [roadmap](../../next-phases.md) and
[artifact runbook](../../runbooks/native-artifacts.md).

The limits are significant: one logical deliverable per task, files up to 1 MiB, independent
model review of supported UTF-8 up to 48,000 bytes, bounded supplied evidence and no general
image/Office-native quality validation. Current byte/nonempty checks do not prove correctness.
The local model declaration does not move inference onto an enrolled runner's machine or
prove a private data boundary. Existing adapters are reusable libraries rather than accepted
native project business tools. Full compute/storage/tool accounting, tenant administration,
hosted operations, restore acceptance and broader live quality remain work.

The prior implementation verification established regression/contract behavior. It did not
run the competitive benchmark, prove live business deliverables or establish a finished SaaS.
Keep **implemented**, **proposed**, **documented by a vendor** and **observed in a comparative
trial** distinct throughout product messaging.

## Five required baseline capabilities

The following tests establish baseline acceptance under the canonical plan. The initial
fixtures can be narrow; the general contracts and declared supported profiles remain
platform scope. These requirements are not conditional on buyer research, novelty or a
30% efficiency gain.

### 1. A native editable delivery packet and result contract (BASE-02)

Translate a messy brief into a short, owner-correctable statement of the intended result:
audience, required facts, formats, checks, responsible decision maker, exclusions and
destination. Preserve native sources, previews, evidence and unresolved limits together.
The value is fewer omissions and faster handoff, not the word “contract.”

Start with supported text and generic/reference file fixtures, then prove each declared
native format by opening, performing an intended edit, saving, reopening and rendering.
The core gate must establish the editable packet contract before an accepted business tool
inherits it. Brief setup and packet maintenance count toward review effort. If receivers
must reconstruct the work or a competent checklist/folder achieves better same-goal
results, redesign the packet and workflow until the prespecified baseline floor is met.

### 2. Accurate change impact and bounded repair (BASE-01)

Bind important facts and requirements to the outputs that use them. A material change
proposes the smallest repair scope and highlights unknown dependency coverage. Preserve
historical acceptance while reassessing present applicability and permissions.

Start with explicit typed fields and recorded dependencies, not a universal semantic graph.
Test irrelevant edits, changed units, contradictions, revoked access and hidden dependencies.
A model may propose impact; it cannot suppress required authority checks. If missed material
effects persist or false stale alerts dominate, redesign coverage and repair scope; coarse
re-review can be an honest bounded implementation where its effort and correctness meet
the declared floor. The obligation to handle changes safely remains.

### 3. Effective owner review without hiding defects (BASE-03)

Show what changed, which checks actually ran, why a finding matters and the next decision.
Keep missing/truncated evidence and unsupported formats visible. Admit work at a rate the
owner can review, and escalate non-converging repairs.

Compare the same outputs in review experiences and strong same-goal configurations, timing
active owner work and blindly assessing hidden defects. Prespecify the review-quality and
effort parity floor per workflow. **30% less active supervision is an optional research
stretch target**, not a core prerequisite, user requirement or universal advantage claim.
A fast approval click with more false acceptance fails acceptance and requires redesign.

### 4. Reliable delivery at honest total cost (BASE-04)

For each supported operation, bind exact input/output, account, destination, authority,
budget and observable receipt. Preserve unknown outcomes and prevent replay from creating
duplicate effects. Prove handoff and interruption recovery on one operation before adding
another. Undo has operation-specific limits; it is not universal rollback.

Measure all unsuccessful attempts, runtime/storage, tool bills, human review, support and
reconciliation. Current unknown calls retain execution slots even on zero-fee models; intake
reserves two calls against a default concurrency of two. Test workspace starvation and
recovery effort before promising effortless cheap autonomy. Do not release uncertain holds
merely to improve a dashboard.

### 5. Authorized continuity across people, tools and providers (BASE-05)

Let another authorized person or runtime continue a supported project with its native files,
decisions and unresolved obligations. Keep current permissions distinct from historical
decisions. Revocation must cover derived content, previews, memory and exports; an old
approval does not grant a new recipient access or action rights.

Prove one maker-to-reviewer-to-receiver path and one provider handoff, then validate the
declared supported profiles. Expensive reconstruction or revoked-information disclosure
blocks acceptance and requires repair; authorized continuity remains mandatory. Local/private deployment is
a separate capability profile requiring verified data paths, supported hardware and support;
it is not a consequence of BYOK or an optional runner.

All five belong to the base platform and reinforce each other. Build and validate them in
bounded slices while preserving the complete gate. The first fixtures join a short
contract, changed evidence, correction and timed owner review; native-file and authorized
handoff profiles complete the baseline evidence. Business tools follow individually after
the accepted core, each inheriting these requirements.

## Where a durable advantage might come from

Baseline parity is a required floor. The following accumulated advantages remain separate
hypotheses beyond that floor; no optional mechanism replaces a baseline obligation.

Most visible UI features are copyable. Plausible advantages develop through repeated useful
work: a consented corpus of accepted outcomes and failures; customer-specific requirement
bindings and validator calibration; well-maintained narrow integrations; domain procedures
with expert ownership; and distribution through practitioners who already deliver the job.
These are hypotheses about accumulated value, not assets Simon presently owns.

Do not build the corpus by pooling private client work without consent. Separate factual
requirements from aesthetic preferences and proposed learned rules from approved policy.
Evidence can improve a validator without authorizing a new business action. Portability may
reduce lock-in while increasing trust, but buyers must actually value it.

An incumbent can copy the presentation, add a dependency feature or bundle the service at a
lower price. Simon needs repeatability, reduced onboarding and measured workflow advantage
before the market narrative becomes durable. Distribution and maintenance can matter more
than a distinctive graph schema.

## Historical commercial hypotheses after baseline acceptance

The October 9 alternatives below remain commercial research options. The October 10
decision retains a general platform; choosing a buyer or fixture for an experiment does
not narrow its core requirements or approve a service/vertical pivot.

| Entry hypothesis                         | Buyer and first job                                                              | Advantage to prove                                                   | Reason to reject                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Human-assisted agency/brand delivery     | Agency owner or delivery lead; a recurring source-backed product/campaign packet | Less correction and producer/reviewer time across related outputs    | Value comes entirely from bespoke expert consulting or support defeats margin |
| Acceptance/repair beside existing agents | AI-enabled operations lead; import, check, correct and return one deliverable    | Better completion with less setup than configuring the current stack | Two authorities confuse users or incumbent configuration matches the result   |
| Maintained research dossier              | Specialist/product operator; recurring supplier/market decision packet           | Useful change detection and maintained recommendations               | Decisions seldom repeat or expert interpretation is the sole purchase         |
| Private production companion             | Team with a binding data/application constraint; one supported file workflow     | Verified privacy boundary and usable native results                  | Hardware, quality or support burden outweighs benefits                        |

The first entry fits the stdout pilot and supplies immediate fixtures, but clothing brands
are not automatically the largest or best market. The second may reduce switching.
Evaluate commercial packaging separately from implementation of the general core. Generic
enterprise control planes, regulated assurances, manufacturing
guarantees and universal company operators carry different proof and sales obligations.

Proposed commercial research: six interviews using two recent jobs each, then three paid
repeat pilots if the relevant work and spending are authorized. These are practical screening
sizes, not statistically representative demand estimates. Identify who pays, what they
already buy, error frequency, active review time, switching friction, reuse and support.
No interviews, sales, contacts or paid competitor accounts were performed during this pass.

Price the complete supported service. A transparent offer can separate platform fee,
included capacity, third-party usage, correction limits and optional expert review. Test
project/milestone packages against recurring workflow subscriptions; avoid unlimited work.
Pure outcome billing is not unique, and accepted-result pricing can hide abandoned work.
Use median and tail cost, not a successful model trace, to assess margin.

Acquisition options worth testing are practitioner partnerships, before/after job demonstrations,
receiver-friendly exported packets, supplier-maintained intake profiles and agency-branded
handoff. Review links and marketplaces are not automatic growth loops. Each channel needs
repeat paid use and manageable support, not just invitations or impressive demos.

## Build, integrate and defer

Retain Simon's authority, exact work identities, acceptance and accounting contracts. Reuse
proven file libraries/application validators and existing customer tools when that removes
work without creating competing authority. Select a durable-runtime migration only if a
measured orchestration gap justifies its operating cost; the existing PostgreSQL controller
already supplies bounded persistence.

Treat deployment and licensing as concrete choices. n8n distinguishes internal use from
external customers configuring hosted workflows; Dify has covered multi-tenant restrictions.
Flowise's official 2026 sunset makes an unexamined hosted foundation inappropriate.
[n8n license guidance](https://support.n8n.io/article/can-i-use-your-license-for-my-use-case),
[Dify license](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/blob/main/LICENSE),
[Flowise sunset](https://flowiseai.com/sunset).

Defer a connector-count race, universal Office/creative editing, a model-training program,
an open agent marketplace, enterprise-wide control towers and unlimited autonomous repair.
These can be future avenues if a buyer and proof obligation emerge. An existing specialist
or human-assisted handoff may be the better answer for some tasks.

## Innovation hypotheses beyond the required baseline

The [100-idea catalog](opportunity-map.md) spans twenty themes rather than one feature list.
Three independent challenge notes add commercial, quality and systems mechanisms; some
overlap intentionally. Their October 9 classification is superseded where the owner has
promoted a requirement. The remaining longer-term experiments include:

1. **Verified procedure compilation.** Explore independently checkable compilation of
   requirements into production procedures. Accurate source/requirement change handling
   already belongs to the baseline; this more ambitious implementation is a hypothesis.
2. **A release rehearsal.** Simulate a few supported destinations, approvals and uncertain
   responses before release. Show what the model does not cover; judge whether rehearsal
   predicts real connector failure, rather than promising rollback.
3. **Branches and reversible learning.** Explore alternate production directions, reviewed
   lessons, held-out replay and merging. Ordinary authorized handoff and usable historical
   records already belong to the baseline; generalized learning and branch semantics do not.

Other avenues remain mapped: capacity planning, accessibility, multilingual work, privacy,
expert review, maintained workflow packs, budgets, agency portfolios, source licensing,
local applications and business simulations. Their inclusion is exploration, not acceptance
of engineering scope. Physical inspection, supplier judgment and domain expertise remain
human or separately validated workflows.

## What remains uncertain and why this pass ends here

No finite pass can explore every conceivable tool or future product. This package covers
the material competitor families, twenty opportunity dimensions, alternative commercial
routes and the strongest found counterexamples. Independent challenges changed the leading
recommendation, and a final source audit corrected current capabilities. That is the useful
coverage boundary for this desk-research pass.

The decisive unknowns now require evidence beyond more browsing: buyer willingness to pay,
configured rival behavior, measured review quality, reliable semantic impact, native-file
fidelity, permission-safe handoff and tail operating costs. Missing public documentation is
not a competitor failure. Future vendor updates can invalidate this dated map.

Start new research when it could change a comparison baseline, build-versus-integrate
decision, commercial hypothesis or acceptance requirement. The next implementation
iteration completes and compares the required core; buyer evidence can inform later
packaging without making baseline quality optional.
